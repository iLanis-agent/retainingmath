Retaining Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts blocks with multiplication and rounding-up: the courses and blocks a wall face takes, the cap units for the run, the gravel a base trench holds, and the block-plus-cap cost per face square foot. The lengths, the block size, the trench and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own pallet tags. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your slope and does not know your soil, your surcharge, your drainage, your geogrid needs or your local wall-height rules, and it gives no verdict that a wall stands. Every number here is your own entry.

The courses truth

Courses and blocks: the wall face divided by the block size, each rounded up.

The caps truth

Cap units: the run divided by the cap length, rounded up, and what is left of the last one.

The gravel truth

Base gravel: the trench length times width times depth, in cubic feet and cubic yards.

The cost truth

Cost: the blocks and caps at their prices, and what that is per face square foot.
